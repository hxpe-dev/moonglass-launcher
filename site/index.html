00 / Moonglass

One full-screen shelf for your Steam, Epic, GOG, Battle.net, Microsoft Store and emulated games, made to be browsed from the couch with a controller. It starts in under a second, and while you play, it does nothing at all.

01 / What it does

Every game you own.
Nothing you don't.

01

Every store, one shelf

Steam, Epic, GOG, Battle.net and Microsoft Store games are found on their own, along with your emulators' ROM folders and any program you add.

02

Made for a controller

Every screen works with a gamepad, setup and search included. Keyboard and mouse work too, and the hints follow what you hold.

03

The stores stay in charge

Games start through Steam, Epic, GOG Galaxy or Battle.net, so achievements, cloud saves and Steam Input keep working.

04

Quiet while you play

Nothing is drawn while a game runs. Quit and Moonglass is back in about a second; hold both sticks in to call it up any time.

05

Artwork without effort

Covers come from your stores' own caches and catalogs, or from SteamGridDB. Set your own for any game.

06

Set it up your way

Posters, banners or squares. Each game's colors, its art behind everything, or pure black for OLED screens.
